Make the desktop usable on tablets

- Touch now covers every core desktop gesture: icons and Finder items open classic-Mac style (tap to select, tap again to open), a long-press opens the right-click menu on browsers that never send `contextmenu` for touch (iOS Safari), and dragging or resizing windows no longer loses the gesture to the browser's pan.
- The small-screen wall now judges coarse-pointer devices by the device, not the current orientation, so rotating a tablet to portrait no longer replaces a running session with the "open this on a laptop" page. Phones stay walled in both orientations — including landscape phones that used to slip past the old width-only check.
- With a menu open, tapping another menu on touch now switches to it instead of closing it.
- The wall copy now says a tablet works, landscape best.

Computer Store aisle boxes shrink to fit the shelf

With six titles in an aisle the centered shelf row overflowed the store window on both sides, and the closeup's clipping made the outermost boxes unclickable — TV Guide in ENTERTAINMENT couldn't be picked up at the default window size. Boxes now scale down (like the counter and detail views already do) so every title on the shelf stays reachable.

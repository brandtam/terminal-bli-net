▚ TERMINAL OS — SIX APP CONCEPTS ▞

Exploration round · July 1986^H^H2026 · click DEMO to play each one█

System review notes: review.md

FOR THE RETRO ENTHUSIAST

▓GlyphDraw

ANSI/ASCII art studio in the spirit of TheDraw. 16-color character-cell canvas, block characters, keyboard-first.

★ Hook: “transmission playback” replays your art like it’s arriving at 2400 baud.

DEMO PRD

♪MicroTracker

4-channel chiptune tracker — pulse, triangle, noise. Hex pattern rows, keyboard piano, songs saved as module files.

★ Hook: scrolling pattern rows + a banging demo tune, born to be screen-recorded.

DEMO PRD

☎The Dialer

Fictional dial-up BBS universe, straight out of 1987. Synthesized modem handshake, message boards, hidden phone numbers to hunt.

★ Hook: the handshake screech — then “I found the secret BBS.”

DEMO PRD
FOR THE TOUCHSCREEN GENERATION

🥚Pixel Pal

A chunky pixel creature that lives in your computer. Feed it, play with it, watch it evolve over real days. It remembers you.

★ Hook: personality moments — zoomies, snores, and “my pal turned into THIS.”

DEMO PRD

🐢Turtle Garden

LOGO turtle graphics as a toy. Type simple commands, the turtle draws, and your program builds itself in an editable pane. First taste of code.

★ Hook: three typed lines bloom into a hypnotic galaxy.

DEMO PRD

📷1-Bit Booth

A photo booth that sees the world like a 1987 computer. Live webcam dithered to 1-bit or Game Boy green, stamps, photo strips.

★ Hook: “this is what cameras used to see??” — dithered selfies, instantly shareable.

DEMO PRD
